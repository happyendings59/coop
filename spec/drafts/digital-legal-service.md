## The idea as stated
From the project ideas inbox, 2026-11-23: "Digital legal service (gov public key, signed notice inbox, served after ~30 days)."

First written in 2024 at https://kwm59.io/nft/ (Feb 20, 2024): government services as expiring or burnable NFTs, legal notices served on chain, tax and vehicle registration tokens, a Chicago Wheel Tax token that burns when it lapses.
